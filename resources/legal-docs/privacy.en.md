## Information we collect

We collect customer name, phone, email, LINE profile data when used, order details, ticket holder names, payment method, payment slip, QR scan logs, and notification preferences.

## How we use data

We use data to process orders, verify payments, issue tickets, support event entry, send event updates, prevent fraud, and improve customer support.

## Sharing

We share information only with event staff, payment reviewers, service providers, or legal authorities when needed to operate the ticketing service or comply with law.

## Your choices

You may request help updating contact details, unsubscribe from optional notifications, or ask about your order data through the event support channel.
