## Using TicketFlow

TicketFlow helps customers reserve event tickets, submit payment evidence, receive digital ticket QR codes, and check in at the venue.

## Orders and payment

Orders remain pending until the event team reviews and approves payment. Customers must provide accurate buyer details, ticket holder names, and payment slip information.

## Ticket use

Each ticket QR is valid only for the named holder and event. Sharing, duplicating, reselling, or tampering with a ticket may lead to refused entry.

## Service changes

Event details, schedules, policies, ticket availability, and operational rules may change when needed. Important updates may be sent through available customer contact channels.
