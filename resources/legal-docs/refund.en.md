## Before approval

If payment cannot be verified or the order is rejected, the event team may contact you for correction or refund handling.

## After approval

Approved tickets are generally non-refundable unless the event organizer states otherwise, the event is cancelled, or local law requires a refund.

## Event changes

If an event is postponed, rescheduled, or materially changed, refund or transfer options depend on the organizer policy for that event.

## How to request

Contact the event support team with your order number, phone number, payment slip, and reason. Refund timing depends on payment channel and bank processing.
